

### SCREEN CAPTURES ###

There are two methods for taking screen captures on this machine: 
  -  Launching a screen capture app from the menus
  -  Using one of three key combinations
  

## Menu Method
  
Launch the xfce4-screenshooter app from either of the two menus: 
  -  The Openbox menu, under Graphics > Screenshot
  -  The Rofi menu (Win + space)  
  

## Key Combo Method

xfce4-screenshooter also has options that let you take different kinds of screen captures. I assigned keybindings in the Openbox config file ( ~/.config/openbox/rc.xml ) that, when triggered, run xfce4-shooter with the appropriate option. 

The three types of screen captures are: 
  -  Alt + Shift + s captures the entire screen
  -  Alt + Shift + w captures the active window
  -  Alt + Shift + d captures a user-defined region
